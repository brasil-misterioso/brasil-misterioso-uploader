Privacy Policy — Brasil Misterioso Uploader

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Brasil Misterioso Uploader ("the tool") is a personal tool used only by the owner of the TikTok account @obrasilmisterioso to publish the owner's own videos to that account through the TikTok Content Posting API.

1. Data the tool accesses

When the account owner authorizes the tool with Login with TikTok, it receives:

  • basic account information (user.info.basic): display name and open ID, used only to confirm which account is connected;
  • permission to publish videos (video.publish) to the owner's own account.

    • The tool does not access data from any other TikTok user, does not read followers, messages, comments or analytics, and does not collect data from viewers.

2. How data is stored

The access token is stored only on the owner's personal computer, in a private local file, and is used only to upload the owner's videos. No data is sent to third parties, sold or shared.

3. Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services). Revoking access, or deleting the local token file, removes all data held by the tool.

4. Children

The tool is not directed to children and collects no data from children.

5. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest version.
